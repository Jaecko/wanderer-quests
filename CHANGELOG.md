# Wanderer Quests 0.1.0

First release, an extension of Wanderer (0.40 or later).

### New
- Every quest of your class, of every dungeon and raid and of every zone, in one Wanderer window with three tabs: your class (by bands of levels), dungeons and raids, zones (the one you stand in chosen first).
- Each place says its levels and how many of its quests you have done; its quests as a tree, the ones that open others above them, wherever they start, in the colors of their difficulty.
- Where you stand with each quest: done, in your log, to take, after another, another choice taken, too high for now, passed (a quest the game let you skip). Only the quests for your race, class and side.
- A quest's sheet beside the window: what to do and its story, in your language; what it asks, items with how many you carry, creatures or objects to find; who gives it and who takes it back, with a pin on your map for each. Read ahead: a quest still far off is prepared as well as one in your log.
- What a quest asks, in one click, to Wanderer's reminders (with how many you carry as you gather it) or to the shopping list of Wanderer Auction.
- The entrance of each dungeon pinned on your map.
- No other quest addon needed: the data comes with it (from the CMaNGOS classic database and pfQuest), read only when you first open the window, only your language loaded.
- Opened from Wanderer's minimap menu, its options page, or /wquests. The window is resizable and keeps its size.
