# Wanderer Quests 1.2.0

Needs Wanderer 1.4 or later. Optional: Wanderer Maps, Wanderer Auction.

### New
- The quests you follow, on your screen, in Wanderer's own list (an option, on): it takes the place of the game's list, and steps aside for Questie's tracker or Kaliel's Tracker.
- One quest in front, at the top: bigger, with a star and its objectives always shown. A click puts a quest in front, a click again lets it go; nothing else ever changes it.
- The game follows the quest in front on its map, even when one of its objectives changes. A quest you choose on the game's map becomes the one in front.
- Your own order, kept for each character: drag a quest by its handle, or Up, Down, To the top, To the bottom from its right click. "Arrange now" orders the list once, by level, by distance or ready to hand in first; nothing moves by itself afterwards.
- Grouped by zone (on): the zone you stand in first, the dungeons apart, each zone folded as you left it. A quest just taken comes at the bottom, marked "New" for a few minutes.
- Clear objectives: the count at the right, a check once done. A quest ready says to whom it goes, and a click shows the way.
- A quest's item on its line, a highest height with the wheel, a compact mode (only the quest in front detailed), faded at rest and quieter in a fight, a light background and a size of your choice. Placed with Wanderer's placing mode, by default where the game's list stood.
- Right click on a quest: its sheet, the way to hand it in, a link in your chat box, share with the group, stop following, abandon (asked first). Shift click stops following it.
- Quests to take on the world map: the game's "!" at each giver with a quest you could take now, from 3 levels below yours to 3 above (on). "Lower quests too" adds the older ones, greyed (off). While a quest's sheet is open, the other givers fade.
- A Books tab: the books of the libraries of WoW Forever, where each lies, where you stand with it, how many you have handed in and the next reward, your librarian on the map.
- With Wanderer Maps: "Go there" and "Add to the route" beside every place; a "Quest route" through the givers of the quests you may take and those who take back the ones you carry, each the nearest from the last; a route through the books still to find.
- Every place is a click away on a sheet: click to follow it, Ctrl click to add it to the route, Shift click for a map link in your chat box (never sent).
- A search by name, from every tab at once.
- "Keep stories for later" (off): the story of a quest you have not taken yet stays folded, so you hear it from its giver first.
- WoW Forever's own quests are learned when you meet their giver ("Seen at ..."), then shown in the lists and the search.

### Changed
- Each quest line has a sign, a chip and its level; filters All, To take, In my log, and "Hide done" (on, kept for each character). Each place sums up its levels and its counts.
- The sheet: where you stand and the levels at the top, its step in the chain, a "Ready?" line (your level, the quests before, what you carry), one main action that follows you (go to the giver, to the objective, hand it in), the creatures that drop what it asks.
- The dungeon and raid entrances are their doors themselves, placed from the world.
- The places of NPCs and objects on the maps WoW Forever redrew (Stormwind, Mulgore, the Eastern Plaguelands, Redridge) are found from where they stand in the world.
- The tabs, scroll bar and colors of Wanderer's other windows; a place, an entrance or a book is followed by Wanderer Maps' arrow when it is there, else by the game's own pin.

### Fixed
- The sheet no longer asks the server again and again for an item it refuses.
- The Gear tab's sheets follow the window's width, and their lines never run over the next.
- A quest accepted is learned whatever the client's event order.
