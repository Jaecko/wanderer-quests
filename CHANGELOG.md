# Wanderer Quests 1.2.43

### New
- The quest menu (tracker, window, map) offers to view the quest in the game's quest log.
- More than a thousand quests of WoW Forever join the lists, with their givers and where to find what they ask, on Forever's own maps.
- Many more items asked by quests now say who drops them, sells them or holds them.
- A Secrets tab: the chains of quests started by an object of the world (a note, a plank, a figurine...), step by step, with the way through them.

### Changed
- On the map, each creature or object a quest asks for has its own zone, where it really stands: four creatures in four places, four zones, no longer one blur over the whole zone. The trail a wandering creature leaves is no longer drawn.
- Quests WoW Forever no longer offers leave the lists and the map.
- Who may take a quest follows WoW Forever's own records more closely: what must be done before it (Forever changed some), a profession at its rank, a standing with a faction, the last level it is offered at, a quest that must be in your log. A quest waiting for a profession or a standing says which, under a new "Not open to you now".
- In a fight, the other quests of the tracker stay readable (less faded), their counts with them; a group's fold mark is a chevron that turns down when open.
- In the tracker, objectives still to do are as bright as the titles and done ones turn a quiet green with a larger check: told apart at a glance.
- In a fight, the other quests stay readable; a quest not finished shows the game's grey sign in the window; the tracking limit is said; sharing or abandoning no longer changes the game log's choice.
- One menu of quest actions everywhere: the tracker's right click, the Quests list (new right click) and the atlas.
- Abandoning a quest names the items it will destroy.
- Closed quests are easier to read, the search and the sources card say when there are more, the window plays the quest log sounds.
- "Pin" and "Add to the shopping list" said the same way as in the other addons.

### Fixed
- A quest you can complete from afar says so in the tracker, and a click completes it, as the game's tracker does.
- Tracker: an objective that becomes undone again (an item dropped or sold) loses its check.
- Quests a giver did not offer you are judged at your current level, even before the quest window was opened.
- Forgetting a character also forgets what givers did not offer it.
- A quest taken or handed in under another number (Forever's own, or the same quest for another race) no longer leaves its twin from the same giver shown as one to take, on the map and in the lists.
- A quest its giver never offered you waits while you still carry one of that giver's (Forever gives it after them): no more giver shown on the map when you have taken everything there.
- Such a quest also waits while that giver still offers you one of the same level or below (Howin Kindfeather in the Wetlands: his later quests no longer show before his first ones are done).
- A quest handed in asks its giver's other quests again (those not offered before): the next of a chain shows without waiting for a new level.
- A breadcrumb is no longer shown to take once you have taken the quest it leads to, nor a quest once you have taken the one after it; a quest whose breadcrumb you carry waits until you hand that one in.
- A quest before another counts as done when you did its rival instead (one quest of a choice).
- Shift and a click on a quest of the tracker always puts its link in the chat, opening the box if need be (no longer following it is in the right click's menu).
- The tracker puts away only the game's quests: its achievements, scenarios and other objectives stay.
- A failed quest says so in red; a timed quest shows its time left; tags for elite, dungeon and raid quests ([30+], [30d], [60r]).
- The quest in front stays in front when you follow a pin on the map.
- Shift+click works as in the game: a link while you write in the chat, otherwise it follows or stops following the quest.
- Item squares and the quest item button: Shift links, Ctrl tries on.
- The next quest of a chain takes the place of the one in front more reliably.
- The quests Forever added show their objective and story in your language once the game has given them (offered by a giver, or in your log), for all your characters.
