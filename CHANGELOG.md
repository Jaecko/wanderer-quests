# Wanderer Quests 1.2.18

### New
- Controller support: the game's shortcut to its quest list (L1 + R1 + up) and the radial wheel's quest entry open Wanderer Quests' tracker. The cross goes from title to title, the bottom button chooses, the left button (square) opens the Atlas or the game's map, the right button goes back. Quest item buttons work with the controller. Needs Wanderer 1.4.36 or later.

### Fixed
- The order of your quests and the quest in front are kept when the quest log is not read yet at login.
- Reading a quest's story opens the right one.
