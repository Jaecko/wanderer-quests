# Wanderer Quests 1.2.26

### Fixed
- A quest its giver does not offer you (Forever closed or changed it) is no longer shown to take, on the map or in the lists, once you have spoken to that giver; asked again when you go up a level, or as soon as it is offered.

### Changed
- A finer look across the family: one palette for every color, clearer contrast on dark windows and on parchment, the same words in every language.
- Under the controller, the tracker's placing button is no longer shown (it needs a mouse); it comes back with the mouse.
