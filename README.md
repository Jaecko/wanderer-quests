# Wanderer Quests

Every quest of your class, your dungeons and your zones, in one quiet Wanderer window, and the quests you follow on screen. Made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 1.4 or later.

- Every quest of your class, of every dungeon and raid and of every zone, each with the quests that lead to it, as a tree; where you stand with each, a search by name, filters.
- A quest's sheet: where you stand, a "Ready?" line, one main action (go to the giver, the objective, hand it in), what it asks and who drops it, its rewards judged for you, what comes next.
- The quests you follow, in Wanderer's own list in place of the game's: one quest in front that the game follows on its map, your own order, zones you can fold, calm at rest and in a fight. It steps aside for Questie's tracker or Kaliel's Tracker.
- The quests you could take on the world map, at their givers; dungeon doors pinned on your map.
- A Gear tab with the best piece your quests can give; a Books tab for the library books of WoW Forever.
- What a quest asks, to Wanderer's reminders or to the shopping list of [Wanderer Auction](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-auction).
- With [Wanderer Maps](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-maps): a place followed by its arrow or added to the route, a "Quest route" through a place's quests, a route through the books.

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-quests). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
