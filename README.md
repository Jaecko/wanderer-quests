# Wanderer Quests

The quests the world hides from you, in one quiet Wanderer window, made for World of Warcraft Forever.
An extension of [Wanderer](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer): it needs Wanderer 0.40 or later.

- Every quest of your class, of every dungeon and raid and of every zone, each with the quests that lead to it, as a tree.
- Where you stand with each: done, in your log, to take, after another, another choice taken, too high for now, passed.
- A quest's sheet: what to do and its story, what it asks (with how many you carry), who gives it and who takes it back, a pin on your map for each.
- What a quest asks, to Wanderer's reminders or to the shopping list of [Wanderer Auction](https://github.com/Jaecko/wanderer-auction).

Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wanderer-quests). More on [wanderer-addon.com](https://wanderer-addon.com).
