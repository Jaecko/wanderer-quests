# Wanderer Quests: credits

Wanderer Quests is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see LICENSE).

Its quest data (QuestData.lua, SpawnData.lua and Names_*.lua) is generated from:

- The CMaNGOS classic database, https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db (GNU GPL v3): the quests, their dungeon,
  the quests before and after them, their levels, races and classes.
- pfQuest by Shagu, https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest (MIT licence, below): who gives each quest, where they
  stand, and the names of the quests and the givers in nine languages.

The dungeon and raid doors (Data.lua, ENTRANCES: their places in the world) come from Endless Map
(AzerothUnrolled) by Hadlock (GNU GPL v3 or later), itself from the MaNGOS and AzerothCore databases.

The places of the NPCs and objects on the maps WoW Forever redrew (Stormwind, Mulgore, the Eastern
Plaguelands, Redridge) are their spawn positions in the CMaNGOS database.

The books of the libraries (LibraryData.lua: each book's quest, item, level and places, the rewards, the
librarians) come from Wowdee, https://wowdee.com (MIT licence, as its .toc states; below).

World of Warcraft and its content are trademarks and copyrights of Blizzard Entertainment.
